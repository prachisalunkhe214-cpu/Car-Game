# 🏎️ Car Racing — 2D Arcade Game

> **Dodge. Race. Survive. Beat your high score.**

A fast-paced 2D arcade racing game built with **Python and Pygame**. 
Take control of your car, avoid incoming traffic, collect rewards, unlock new cars, and try to survive as long as possible!

## ✨ Features

🎮 **Smooth Gameplay**  
Control your car and navigate through constantly changing traffic.

🚗 **Multiple Cars**  
Unlock and select different cars as you progress.

💰 **Coin & Reward System**  
Earn in-game currency while playing and use it to unlock new content.

🏆 **High Score**  
Challenge yourself to beat your previous best score.

💥 **Collision System**  
One wrong move and it's game over!

🔊 **Sound & Music**  
Background music and crash sound effects make the gameplay more interactive.

💾 **Save System**  
Your progress, money, selected car, and high score are stored using JSON.

🔄 **Restart System**  
Quickly restart the game and try to beat your previous score.

🎁 **Reward System**  
Claim rewards and unlock additional gameplay content.

## 🕹️ Controls

| Key | Action |
|:---:|---|
| ⬅️ | Move Left |
| ➡️ | Move Right |
| ⬆️ | Move Up |
| ⬇️ | Move Down |
| `SPACE` | Select / Start |
| `ESC` | Exit |

---

## 🏁 How to Play

1. Start the game.
2. Choose your car.
3. Hit the road!
4. Avoid incoming vehicles.
5. Keep driving to increase your score.
6. Collect money and unlock new cars.
7. Try to beat your **High Score**.
8. Crash? No problem — hit restart and race again! 🏎️💨
